## Proposed pilot offer

**One-policy support handoff pilot.** Turn one current policy and a permitted anonymized case set into a tested exception guide, complete review packets and an instrumented local desk. Compare it with the existing flow before proposing any merchant workflow change.

The proposed buyer is an eligible small apparel merchant owner or support lead with recurring exceptions. No buyer relationship is established. Include one merchant, one policy version, the local desk, source register, one correction cycle and an effort/quality receipt. Account operation, messages, labels, refunds, payments, inventory changes and public deployment are outside this prepared offer.

Price is unquoted: delivery effort, correction load and willingness to pay are unobserved. The planning allowance is one thirty-minute policy review and one fifteen-minute sample review; these are proposed founder-attention limits, not agreed or measured work.

## Measurement and decision agreement

The [machine-readable pilot](../../packages/foundry/test/fixtures/commercial-release/pilot.v1.json) is a draft agreement, not an accepted engagement.

Start with the disclosed local scenarios and competent manual policy/checklist comparator. Record actual supported routing, missing required information, unsupported promises, corrections, preparation seconds, review seconds and receipt identity. All initial measurements are null or not run. Deterministic persistence checks can establish mechanics; synthetic cases cannot establish saved customer labor.

A later consenting-operator pilot could cover ten eligible anonymized cases or ten business days, whichever comes first. This is a descriptive learning window, not a statistical power calculation. Check instrumentation before interpreting outcomes. Keep native routing for ordinary cases and record whether extra preparation adds work.

Advance only after an eligible operator identifies a worthwhile unmet need and measured task work improves without a material policy error. Continue when specified information is missing; repair and retest demonstrated defects while retaining the original result. Stop or retain the incumbent process if the desk adds effort without useful benefit or needs unavailable authority. A real customer observation remains awaiting feedback until it exists.

## Delivery economics

Keep cash collected, paid runtime expense, other direct expense, setup, delivery, correction, maintenance and owner-review time separate. Every initial input in the pilot packet is null.

- Direct delivery contribution equals cash collected minus paid runtime/other direct costs and explicitly priced delivery/correction labor.
- Full pilot cost also includes setup and maintenance labor.
- A future support-labor scenario needs observed eligible case volume, minutes saved per case and the operator's labor rate.
- Return unknown if any required input is unknown. Unpaid owner time is not zero-cost labor.

A local receipt is not a sale. Preserved gross sales, refunds, contribution and net profit are different measures. No profit, savings or revenue estimate is supported by this packet.
