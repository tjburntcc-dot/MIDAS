# Worker development loop

`WorkerDevelopment` is the prospective worker-improvement boundary for a single business and job. A definition exposes its responsibilities, pinned model route, declared tools, permitted evidence, output contract, procedure scope, evaluation history, and known limits. The initial procedure is an excellent generalist baseline. A specialist is optional and is selected only after this job's supported comparison.

The loop is deliberate: record a sourced correction, omission, or outcome failure; diagnose one missing knowledge, tool, context, or procedure cause while preserving alternatives; retain only reusable permitted material; create a versioned candidate and separate practice/evaluation cases; then call `runComparison` with an injected `ModelPort`. The port must already own any admission and accounting through the existing experiment ledger. This module has no credentials, provider implementation, token counting, account access, spending path, or fallback.

`offline_fixture` accepts only a fixture port and remains permanently unqualified. It records an exact four-arm request/response provenance trail for the two required evaluation cases and output hashes to prove plumbing. Its call ceiling is exact: one attempt per arm and exactly two arms for every evaluation case. `ledger_backed_runtime` is intentionally unavailable until a trusted factory binds an exact signed OperatingModels admission and durable ledger attempt to every arm. A caller-supplied signature string, grant-shaped object, custodian name, port, route, or credential cannot authorize it. `comparisonGrantRequirements` creates the unsigned request shape; it does not authorize a call.

Evaluation cases are never used as practice cases. A protected-evaluation claim requires a custodian-sealed manifest and custodian identity. In other cases the comparison is explicitly non-protected and development-visible; it is not described as a heldout evaluation. Model prose is not scored in this module. An external assessor returns opaque grade receipts. A single critical commercial omission rejects the candidate even when aggregate accepted cases favor it. Only the existing `ProcedureRegistry` can admit a supported scoped adoption; otherwise future assignment retains the strong baseline.

When material is withdrawn or becomes stale, `staleEvidence` records the event, marks the candidate unavailable for future assignment, and rolls an adopted procedure back to the baseline when necessary. An owner evidence-selection revocation also blocks fresh comparison dispatch and the future-assignment facade because `PilotKnowledge.selectedSources` is rechecked at dispatch time. Procedure pins and prior evidence remain preserved. Definitions and candidates are business/job scoped, so source IDs from one business cannot enter another business's worker.

`WorkerDevelopmentService` is the UI/service contract for this capability. It exposes `define`, `observe`, `intake`, `prepareDiagnosis`, `propose`, `practice`, `evaluate`, `grade`, `assignment`, and `report`, without changing the shared pilot service, server, or browser app. `prepareDiagnosis` gives a strict model-assisted diagnosis and material-selection schema but is preparation-only until the trusted signed factory exists. Local or assisted candidate proposals stay explicitly unqualified. `assignment` produces a future `PilotLearning`-compatible procedure binding and reports `blocked_source_revoked` when the current owner evidence selection no longer permits the worker's sources.
