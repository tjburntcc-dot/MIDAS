That weakens any generic pitch of “you need an online quote form.” A useful offer must discover an actionable task problem or expensive checking process that their existing tools/webmaster do not solve. Until buyers establish that gap, differentiation and demand remain unproven. Stop feature-building if that distinction cannot be explained from real buyer evidence.

## Bounded manual discovery procedure — prepared, not executed outreach

1. Keep Raleigh as a reversible sample or substitute a place with a genuine permitted access advantage before recruiting.
2. Search public web for Raleigh residential cleaning estimate. Manually inspect at most ten distinct official company websites or two hours, whichever comes first. Deduplicate brands; exclude commercial-only businesses.
3. Record official domain, source date, residential scope, service geography, estimate route, existence of a suitable business-contact channel and unknown ownership. Do not scrape personal contact data, infer owner email addresses or call a website a qualified prospect without verification.
4. Rank for segment fit and ability to act on a report, not an invented website defect. Keep negative fit evidence. A business with adequate checking may be the wrong buyer.
5. Present at most five exact invitations, recipients and business channels for Mason's separate approval. Do not use customer quote forms disguised as cleaning requests. No channel is assumed permissible merely because it is visible.
6. If approved, aim for at most five completed discovery interviews within the stated time bound. No automatic expansion, repeat messaging or replacement list is authorized. If five conversations are not obtained, report that evidence gap.
7. If no appropriate access/channel is found, stop this route. An actual agency introduction could change the ranking; absent access, revise the business hypothesis before buying tools.

## Concrete two-week calendar

Recommended maximum each week: Monday through Friday two hours/day, Saturday five hours, Sunday five hours = twenty hours. Weekday blocks follow school and existing obligations. Weekend totals include breaks. Use Monday/Wednesday for access and buyer evidence; Tuesday for learning; Thursday for delivery/review; Friday for synthesis; Saturday for longer practice/delivery and any separately approved buyer work; Sunday for records, school planning and contingency.

School and normal sleep take priority. This is not confirmed founder availability and not a forty-hour output quota. Cancel or shorten blocks if prerequisites are blocked or school needs time. Unused contingency becomes free time; it must not become extra speculative features. Learning, interview acquisition and corrections count toward effort, even when they produce no revenue.
