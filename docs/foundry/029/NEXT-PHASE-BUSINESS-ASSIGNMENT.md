# Proposed next assignment: supplier invoice exceptions and release recommendation

**Preparation only. No execution, spending, payment or new mission is authorized by this proposal.** Finish and close the W-001/W-006 laboratory observations before considering it. Do not consume remaining Mission 029 headroom on this work.

## Business assignment and usable deliverable

Given a synthetic small distributor's eight-invoice accounts-payable batch, prepare the next payment-release recommendation under a cash limit. Obtain missing receiving evidence; reconcile purchase orders, supplier invoices, delivery records and credit notes; identify duplicates and unresolved discrepancies; prioritize eligible obligations; produce an auditable packet for the finance owner. Publish only the exact approved internal packet to the fixture. No bank instruction, vendor communication or actual payment is in scope.

Deliverable: a line-item release/hold register, proposed amounts in integer currency units, source references, an exception list with the specific next evidence request and responsible human, remaining cash, and a short owner-facing decision memo. The packet should be usable for reviewing the batch without reconstructing the agent's conversation.

This tests a material gap beyond the support laboratory: joining several records, resolving partial fulfillment and superseded evidence, identifying duplicate economic obligations, allocating scarce cash, and maintaining a line-item audit trail through correction and verified publication. W-001 established ordinary delivery; W-006 tests a disclosed draft defect and deterministic response-loss recovery. Neither establishes these reconciliation or financial-control competencies.

## Fixed first test and ground truth

Prepare three developer-visible synthetic batches using invented organizations and rights-cleared records. Each has eight invoices with a bounded corpus of purchase orders, invoices, receiving records, credit notes, supplier terms and cash availability. Cover straightforward release, partial receipt, duplicate invoice, missing receiving evidence obtainable by a permitted lookup, explicit policy supersession, credit allocation, and a justified hold. These are synthetic cases, not customer validation or a protected holdout.

Every operating definition must appear in worker-visible policy: receipt eligibility, exact versus tolerance matching, duplicate identity, currency, credit applicability, release priority/tie-break, treatment of unresolved evidence and the scope of approval. Missing records must have an authorized retrieval path or an explicit unavailable result. Do not hide a mandatory rule in the grader. Before any call, independently work the small batches by hand and deterministic arithmetic; flag ambiguous records rather than manufacture a model error.

Ground truth is the generated source ledger plus an independently checked expected release/hold register. Worker inputs exclude that register and evaluator labels. Objective checks independently recompute line matching, arithmetic, cash limits, duplicate obligations, evidence currency, authority and fixture state. Semantic usefulness of explanations remains a separately labeled reviewer judgment. Preserve incorrect, incomplete and blocked outcomes.

## Strong baseline and possible team

Start with an excellent single workflow owner: full authorized corpus/tools, explicit reconciliation checklist, current operating policy, persistent task state, and a real review/correction call. The baseline should already receive sound instructions about source precedence, duplicates, partial deliveries, arithmetic and uncertainty.

The smallest plausible team is a workflow owner plus a reconciliation verifier. The owner gathers evidence and drafts the register; the verifier independently recomputes proposed lines from the same source records and reviews the actual packet before approval, then inspects delivery. A role title is not qualification. Additional human accounting expertise is required if the synthetic rules leave a real accounting judgment unresolved.

Keep model/settings, source/tool access, four-call aggregate ceiling, token/exposure limits, correction opportunity, approval and independent acceptance identical. The treatment is a source-first reconciliation procedure and authorship separation, not privileged information or extra computation. Store any team recommendation separately from fixed assignments. Do not run the team if its actual request/procedure differs only cosmetically or if no result could change the allocation decision.

Four-call allocation: (1) identify missing decision-relevant records and issue permitted evidence requests; (2) reconcile and draft the release/hold packet; (3) review and revise the exact packet using the full authorized evidence; (4) inspect the approved published packet, authenticated receipt/readback and obligations. Deterministic retrieval and arithmetic checking use no hidden model calls. Pre-publication correction cannot silently add a fifth call.

## What results would change

- Similar adequate outcomes: prefer the sufficient single configuration; do not pay for nominal independence.
- Team benefit: identify the caught reconciliation failure and its source/procedure mechanism; confirm on new matched batches before adoption.
- Team regression: inspect handoff omissions, unnecessary holds and extra cost before adding workers.
- Shared failure: fix an ambiguous policy, inaccessible record or inadequate checker equally before specializing.
- Inconclusive: name the missing evidence—such as reviewer agreement, representative exceptions or billing reconciliation—and choose the smallest additional test that resolves it.

Measure paired **batch-level** decision correctness, line eligibility, critical unauthorized release/duplicate/cash-limit failures, justified and unnecessary holds, usable artifact completion, actual fixture state, calls/tokens, latency, provisional and reconciled costs, and measured correction effort. Eight lines are related items within a batch, not eight independent experiments. Three synthetic batches cannot establish broad superiority or payment-system reliability.

The result would inform whether a worker can prepare this bounded reconciliation packet, what source access and human judgment it needs, and whether a separate reviewer earns its cost. It would not establish autonomous finance operation, learned optimal team assembly or general business competence.
