# Atlas Owner Policy: Signal Freshness Clarification

Synthetic owner-authored policy for development evaluation. Not real customer policy.

## Scope
This revision clarifies how stale evidence, current disqualifiers, and research-needed conflicts are distinguished. It does not replace earlier freshness rules.

## Rules
- A stale first-party hiring signal is insufficient to establish current hiring and requires research; it is not an automatic disqualification.
- A current first-party disqualifier such as a hiring freeze can exclude the account even when older third-party listings remain.
- When first-party and third-party hiring evidence conflict, or when current first-party hiring status is unknown, research is required.
- Do not treat recency decay or stale-only evidence as a hard exclusion.

## Why it matters
Insufficient stale evidence and a present disqualifier are different decisions.
