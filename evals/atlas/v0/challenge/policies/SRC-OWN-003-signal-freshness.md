# Atlas Owner Policy: Signal Freshness

Synthetic owner-authored policy for development evaluation. Not real customer policy.

## Scope
This policy states how hiring and demand signals may be used.

## Rules
- A first-party hiring signal older than 45 days is stale and does not establish current hiring.
- A current first-party freeze overrides older third-party listings.
- Third-party listings do not establish current first-party hiring on their own.
- When first-party signal age or current open roles are unknown, research is required.
- Do not treat a stale third-party board as proof of a current buying or hiring condition.

## Why it matters
Timing is a ranking feature after hard fit is established, not a substitute for a current first-party condition.
