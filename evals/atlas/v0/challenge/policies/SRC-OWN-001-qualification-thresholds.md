# Atlas Owner Policy: Qualification Thresholds

Synthetic owner-authored policy for development evaluation. Not real customer policy.

## Scope
This policy states reusable qualification thresholds for a founder-led commercial motion.

## Rules
- Serve United States accounts only. Accounts headquartered outside the United States are excluded.
- Require a minimum of eight employees. Headcount below eight is a hard exclusion.
- Require verified monthly spend of at least 2500 USD. Do not infer spend from employee count.
- When employee count or verified monthly spend is unknown, research is required. Do not invent the missing number.
- Structural fit is established from these account properties, not from activity volume.

## Why it matters
Thresholds must stay explicit so interest or a large-sounding team cannot erase a failed size or spend test.
