# Atlas Owner Policy: Buyer Authority

Synthetic owner-authored policy for development evaluation. Not real customer policy.

## Scope
This policy states who may be treated as a contracting buyer.

## Rules
- Require owner authority or written delegation before qualification.
- A job title is not proof of purchasing authority, including director, vice president, and manager titles.
- Office manager, coordinator, and similar roles are not purchasing authority unless written delegation exists.
- Records marked none, headquarters-only, or title-only are excluded.
- When buyer authority is unknown, research is required. Do not invent written delegation.

## Why it matters
Interest and seniority are not substitutes for the right to sign.
