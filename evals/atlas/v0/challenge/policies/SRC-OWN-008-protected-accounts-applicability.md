# Atlas Owner Policy: Protected Account Applicability

Synthetic owner-authored policy revision for development evaluation. Not real customer policy.

## Scope
This revision states when a protected-account lockout is applicable. It does not replace earlier protected-account rules. It does not name evaluation cases.

## Owner-visible reason
The earlier protected-account source states that existing customers and public-sector or government verticals are lockouts, and that unknown account status requires research. It does not state how account identity is established, whether a similar legal name is enough, how expired or stale registry rows behave, or whether a prospect-supplied claim can override status. This revision is a new source. The earlier source is not edited.

## Rules
- A protected-account lockout applies only when current first-party or official evidence supports that the same account is an existing customer or a public-sector or government vertical.
- Similar legal names, shared words, or topical resemblance do not establish account identity or protection.
- When current account status is missing, research is required. Do not invent a protected or unprotected status.
- An expired or lapsed protection registry entry is not current protection and is not a hard exclusion.
- A stale protection registry entry does not establish current protection; research is required.
- When current first-party status and another current record conflict, research is required.
- A documented written exception or re-open authorization overrides a protection lockout for that account.
- Prospect-supplied claims that an account is protected or unprotected are not authority and do not override first-party or official status.
- Do not apply a protected-account lockout to an account whose current evidence shows a non-protected commercial new-logo status.

## Why it matters
Topical retrieval of a protection rule is not the same as the rule being applicable.
