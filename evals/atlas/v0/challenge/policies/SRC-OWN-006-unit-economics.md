# Atlas Owner Policy: Unit Economics

Synthetic owner-authored policy for development evaluation. Not real customer policy.

## Scope
This policy states capacity and payback constraints for a seat-based offer.

## Rules
- Require a minimum of four seats. Accounts below four seats are excluded.
- Exclude accounts whose modeled payback is longer than nine months.
- Seller capacity must cover at least twelve new accounts.
- Do not infer seat count from employee count.
- When seat count is unknown, research is required.

## Why it matters
A large-looking account with a long payback or too few seats still fails the motion.
